## 1. Setup
- **Data:** 250,000 listings, SHA-256 `b0032196fcf33679…`
- **Split:** random, stratified by city: train 175,000 / validation
  37,500 / test 37,500 (seed 42).
  Rows are independent listings with no repeats and no time index, so group- or time-aware validation is not required.
- **Label:** Rupees per sqft at or below 1 x the training-split median of its City x Property_Type x BHK cohort (fallback City, then global, when a cohort has < 30 rows).
- **Class balance:** 50.1% positive (train), 50.0% (test).
- **Selection:** highest validation ROC-AUC on the listing track; ties within 0.001 broken by lower validation Brier score. Test metrics were not used for selection.
